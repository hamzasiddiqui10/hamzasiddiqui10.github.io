Eckerlab | Aug 2026 - Present

Video Generalization Dataset (VGD)

Research project investigating "object bias" in video action recognition. Exploring whether models truly understand temporal actions or simply cheat by recognizing static background objects. To evaluate this, I curated a custom multi-camera dataset with intentional visual distractors and built automated probing pipelines on HPC clusters to benchmark state-of-the-art foundation models (DINOv2, V-JEPA2, SAM3) and large Vision-Language Models.

Side Project | Sept 2020

Simulating Wealth Distribution in Free Market Economies

Simulation that modelled a free market economy with finite wealth, players and voluntary transactions. And I observed how wealth distribution changed over time under different starting conditions for the distribution. The results showed tendency of such systems to converge to highly unequal outcomes wherein most people have very little, and a few have most of the wealth.
